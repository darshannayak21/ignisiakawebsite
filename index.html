Judging Framework

How we evaluate.

A simple guide to how we review and appreciate your creations.

Problem Understanding

Strong solutions begin with a clear understanding of the problem they aim to solve. We evaluate how well teams identify the core challenge, define the scope, and justify why the problem matters.

Innovation & Originality

We look for creative applications of AI and emerging technologies that introduce novel approaches. Projects demonstrating unique thinking, fresh perspectives, and meaningful differentiation score higher.

Technical Feasibility & Implementation

We assess how effectively teams translate concepts into working systems — including technical design, code quality, implementation complexity, and prototype reliability.

Impact, Scalability & Usability

The most compelling projects create real value. We evaluate potential impact, ability to scale, and usability — rewarding solutions with clear real-world relevance and intuitive user experience.
