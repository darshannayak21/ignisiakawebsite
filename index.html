AI Hackathon

India's Premier National AI Hackathon

Ignisia is MIT World Peace University's flagship national artificial intelligence hackathon — a grand stage bringing together India's most extraordinary student innovators, coders, and visionaries. Designed as an intense 24-hour physical building sprint on campus, Ignisia challenges teams to engineer, deploy, and scale groundbreaking AI solutions that address critical real-world problems. From generative systems and autonomous agents to computer vision and intelligent automation, Ignisia is where raw ambition transforms into industry-defining technology.

Core Event · March 2026

Ignisia — National AI Hackathon

Full Description

MIT World Peace University's flagship national AI hackathon — a grand stage bringing together India's most extraordinary student innovators, coders, and visionaries. Teams compete in an intense 24-hour physical building sprint on campus, engineering AI solutions that address critical real-world problems. Evaluation focuses on both technological excellence and positive societal impact.

Key Objectives

  • Provide a premier national platform for AI innovation and applied technical excellence
  • Foster interdisciplinary collaboration to solve complex societal challenges
  • Evaluate projects on technical rigor and real-world societal impact
  • Connect student innovators with mentors, researchers, and industry leaders
  • Establish Ignisia as India's leading student-led AI competition

Key Dates & Highlights

Annual — March Idea Submission: Mar 17, 2026 Screening: Mar 18–22, 2026 24-Hour Grand Finale On-Campus Prize Pool: ₹1 Lakh+

Target Audience & Deliverables

Open to student teams from all recognised educational institutions across India (National Level). Participants deliver a working AI prototype or deployed product solving a real-world problem. Formats include generative AI, autonomous agents, computer vision, NLP, and intelligent automation.

Core Events · April 2026

Technophilia & Innovision — National Tech Fest & Showcase

Full Description

Two powerful events held concurrently: Technophilia is the flagship annual national technical festival — a multi-day celebration of innovation through competitions, conferences, workshops, and exhibitions. Innovision is the national project exhibition and showcase, providing students a platform to demonstrate engineering design, applied research, and technological problem-solving before academicians, industry experts, and the wider community.

Key Objectives

  • Promote technical education, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Provide practical learning and hands-on skill development opportunities
  • Recognise outstanding student innovations through national awards
  • Facilitate academia–industry interaction and research culture
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and product development

Key Dates & Highlights

Annual — March / April Technophilia: Coding Contests & Conferences Innovision Track 1: Capstone Review (3rd Year) Innovision Track 2: National Open Exhibition Separate Awards per Department

Target Audience & Deliverables

Technophilia: Students, researchers, academicians, and industry professionals (national level). Innovision: MIT-WPU final-year Polytechnic students (Track 1) and students from all recognised institutions nationwide (Track 2). Judges include faculty members, academic coordinators, and industry experts.

Core Event · August 2026

I-Talk — Innovation & Leadership Summit

Full Description

The flagship thought-leadership and knowledge-sharing conference of Ignisia Club — organised to inspire students through meaningful interactions with distinguished personalities from academia, industry, entrepreneurship, government, research, media, and sports. Unlike conventional lecture-based conferences, I-Talks emphasise interactive learning, audience participation, open dialogue, and experiential engagement.

Key Objectives

  • Inspire students through real-world experiences and success stories
  • Promote innovation, leadership, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning
  • Facilitate interaction between students and distinguished personalities
  • Expose participants to emerging technologies, industries, and global trends
  • Create networking and mentorship opportunities; foster curiosity and professional growth

Key Dates & Formats

Annual — August / September Keynote Conversations Fireside Chats Panel Discussions Live Demonstrations Mentor Interactions Innovation Showcases Networking Sessions

Target Audience & Deliverables

Open to all Ignisia Club members, students, and the broader MIT-WPU community. Speakers include distinguished personalities from diverse fields. Active audience participation is a core design principle — not a passive lecture experience.

Core Event · October 2026

Incubex — National Startup & Innovation Challenge

Full Description

The flagship entrepreneurship and startup challenge of Ignisia Club. Unlike conventional hackathons or problem-statement competitions, Incubex focuses on the full spectrum of the innovation journey — from raw ideation to registered, operational startups. It functions as a platform connecting innovators with mentors, industry feedback, incubation opportunities, strategic partnerships, and potential investment.

Key Objectives

  • Promote innovation and entrepreneurial thinking among students
  • Support the transformation of ideas into viable products and startups
  • Facilitate interaction between innovators, mentors, investors, and industry leaders
  • Recognise high-potential technologies and business ideas
  • Establish Ignisia as a national platform for student entrepreneurship

Competition Stages

Annual — October / November 1. Ideation 2. Validation 3. Prototype 4. MVP 5. Registered Startup 6. Deep Tech & Research

Target Audience & Deliverables

Open to student innovators and startup founders from all recognised educational institutions. Judges include entrepreneurs, founders, VCs, angel investors, academicians, and subject-matter experts. Format includes pitching, mentor sessions, investor interactions, and exhibitions. In association with incubation centres, accelerators, startup ecosystems, and MIT-WPU.

Educational Session · Year-Round

Workshops & Upskilling Bootcamps

Full Description

The Upskilling Tracks framework is the official ecosystem for technical education, professional development, innovation, research, and continuous learning within Ignisia Club. Structured Upskilling Tracks cover emerging technologies and industry-relevant domains with learning paths, practical assignments, workshops, projects, and assessments. The Club also maintains a central repository of educational resources and formal mentorship programmes.

Key Objectives

  • Promote continuous technical learning and strengthen problem-solving abilities
  • Prepare members for industry careers and academic excellence
  • Support participation in national and international competitions
  • Promote entrepreneurship, product development, and lifelong learning
  • Develop leadership through technical excellence

Tech Domains Covered

AI & Machine Learning Web Design & Development Mobile App Development Cybersecurity Blockchain & Web3 Cloud Computing & DevOps Internet of Things Data Science UI/UX Design

Target Audience & Deliverables

Open to all Ignisia Club members and students throughout the academic year. Learning formats include lectures, practice sessions, workshops, projects, assessments, mentorship, and peer study groups. May include certification programmes in collaboration with academic institutions and industry organisations.

Tech Initiative · Year-Round

DSA & Competitive Programming Initiative

Full Description

The Competitive Programming Programmes are established to enhance algorithmic thinking, programming proficiency, computational problem-solving, and technical interview preparedness among members. The Core Programming & DSA Team manages algorithm design, data structures, competitive programming, contests, optimisation, mentoring, and technical interview preparation. Includes a dedicated ICPC Cell for international-level competition preparation.

Key Objectives

  • Enhance algorithmic thinking and programming proficiency
  • Strengthen computational problem-solving skills
  • Prepare members for technical interviews at top companies
  • Represent the university in national and international programming contests (ICPC)
  • Build a competitive programming culture within Ignisia

Programme Highlights

Year-Round Structured Programme ICPC Cell (Selection + Training Camps) Internal Ranking & Contests Codeforces & LeetCode HackerRank & CodeChef Mock Contests & Peer Mentoring

Target Audience & Topics

Open to all Ignisia members with interest in competitive programming and technical interview preparation. Topics include: Data Structures, Algorithms, Problem Solving, Complexity Analysis, Programming Techniques, Interview Preparation, and Advanced Competitive Programming.

Tech Initiative · Year-Round

Networking & Community Growth

Full Description

The community backbone of Ignisia Club — building lasting connections among students, alumni, faculty, industry professionals, researchers, and policymakers. The Club maintains a multi-platform digital ecosystem (Website, GitHub Organisation, Discord Server, Email Services) and engages its community through I-Talks, mentorship programmes, hackathon support, study groups, and certification initiatives. It also connects innovators with investment and incubation opportunities.

Key Objectives

  • Connect students with researchers, entrepreneurs, industry leaders, policymakers, and alumni
  • Establish and maintain a strong multi-channel digital community presence
  • Foster interdisciplinary collaboration among students from diverse academic backgrounds
  • Promote responsible innovation and the ethical application of technology
  • Develop leadership, professionalism, and organisational excellence

Official Digital Platforms

ignisia.tech (Official Website) GitHub Organisation Discord Server Official Email Services Mentorship Programmes Peer Study Groups Alumni Network Hackathon Support

Target Audience & Deliverables

Targets all Ignisia Club members, potential members, alumni, and industry partners. Delivers an active online community, mentorship connections, alumni network, and industry partnerships. Includes career guidance, technical mentoring, project supervision, research support, startup mentoring, and professional development support.
