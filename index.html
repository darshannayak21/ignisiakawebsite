// 01 — About

Where India's brightest student minds design, build, and deploy cutting-edge AI-driven solutions.

Ignisia is a flagship technical event reinforcing MIT-WPU's commitment to technology, innovation, research, and societal impact. Cross-disciplinary teams compete to build real AI solutions for real-world problems.

National Level
Open to students from universities across India
24-Hour Grand Finale
Non-stop intensive build marathon
Hybrid Format
Online rounds + On-campus Grand Finale
Hosted at MIT-WPU
MIT World Peace University, Pune

// 02 — How It Works

Three Stages.
One Mission.

01
Online
Idea Screening

Submit your project idea and get shortlisted based on innovation, feasibility, and relevance to real-world problems.

02
Online
Online Interviews

Shortlisted teams pitch their concepts to a panel of expert judges who evaluate clarity, depth, and potential.

03
On-Campus · 24 Hours
Grand Finale

Build and deploy your solution in a live, high-pressure hackathon environment at MIT-WPU. Ship or go home.

// 05 — Judging Framework

How we evaluate.

Innovation

Originality and creative application of AI/technology to solve the problem in an unexpected way.

Feasibility

Technical soundness, implementation quality, and realistic path to a working prototype.

Scalability

Potential to grow, reach wider populations, and sustain impact beyond the hackathon.

Social Responsibility

Ethical design considerations and tangible benefit to society, communities, or underserved populations.
