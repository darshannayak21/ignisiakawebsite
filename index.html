Judging Framework

How we evaluate.

A simple guide to how we review and appreciate your creations.

Innovation

Originality and creative application of AI to solve a problem in an unexpected way.

Feasibility

Technical soundness, implementation quality, and a realistic path to a working prototype.

Scalability

Potential to grow, reach wider populations, and sustain impact beyond the hackathon.

Social Impact

Tangible benefit to society, communities, or underserved populations — built responsibly.
